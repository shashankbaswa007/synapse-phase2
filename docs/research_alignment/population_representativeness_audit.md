## 1. Executive Summary

This audit investigates whether the synthetic older-adult evaluation cohort ($N = 575$ individuals aged $\ge 45$) generated by SYNAPSE Phase 2 is representative of its intended target population (community-dwelling older adults in India, corresponding to LASI Wave 1 eligibility), and rigorously distinguishes **population demographic characteristics** from **healthcare utilization and vital sign observation availability**.

### Core Audit Findings
1. **Target Population Demographics are Representative:** The marginal and joint distributions of age and sex across all 2,200 synthetic patients and within the 575 older adults faithfully match the **Census of India SRS 2021** life tables and population pyramids ($\text{JSD} < 0.01$).
2. **Extreme Indication / Healthcare Selection Bias in Blood Pressure Telemetry:** In the current simulator architecture, longitudinal blood pressure observations are emitted **exclusively** within the `hypertension.yaml` disease module. Normotensive older adults ($N = 340$, $59.13\%$) never enter a vital-sign recording state and have zero blood pressure observations in `observations.csv`.
3. **Severe Healthcare Selection Effect:** Consequently, the 235 older adults with measured blood pressure do **not** represent a random sample of synthetic older adults. They represent a **$100\%$ hypertensive outpatient clinical cohort**.
4. **Estimand Conflation:** Directly comparing this measured clinical subset against unselected community survey data (such as LASI Wave 1) conflates a **clinical outpatient diagnostic cohort** with a **general community population**.

## 2. Demographic Generation and Observation Process

We traced synthetic patients from initialization through healthcare encounters and vital sign accumulation:

### 2.1 Patient Demographic Assignment
- **Mechanism:** In [`synapse_simulator.datagen.patient_generator.PatientGenerator`](file:///Users/shashi/synapse/synapse-phase2/synapse_simulator/datagen/patient_generator.py), patient age, sex, date of birth, and baseline chronic conditions are assigned deterministically at $t_0 = \text{2024-01-01T00:00:00}$ using seed 42.
- **Population Profile:** Guided by [`config/population/india_default_v1.yaml`](file:///Users/shashi/synapse/synapse-phase2/config/population/india_default_v1.yaml), reproducing Census SRS 2021 age pyramids:
  * Total Patients: $2,200$
  * Age 0–14: $571$ ($25.95\%$)
  * Age 15–24: $374$ ($17.00\%$)
  * Age 25–44: $680$ ($30.91\%$)
  * Age 45–64: $416$ ($18.91\%$)
  * Age 65+: $159$ ($7.23\%$)
  * Total Older Adults ($\ge 45$): $575$ ($26.14\%$)
  * Female Older Adults: $276$ ($48.00\%$), Male Older Adults: $299$ ($52.00\%$).

### 2.2 Entry into Clinical Encounters
Every synthetic patient has a defined simulation start ($t_0 = \text{2024-01-01T00:00:00}$). However, clinical encounters are generated through two distinct mechanisms:
1. **Module-Driven Encounters:** Discrete-event disease modules schedule encounters when clinical state transitions trigger an `Encounter` state.
   - In `hypertension.yaml`: Baseline-positive patients immediately enter `ScreeningEncounter`. Incident cases enter after `HypertensionOnset` + 90-day `AsymptomaticDelay`.
   - In `diabetes_t2.yaml`: Baseline-positive patients enter `DiagnosticEncounter`. Incident cases enter after onset.
2. **Acute Episodic Encounters:** Outpatient and emergency encounters for acute illnesses (e.g. Upper Respiratory Tract Infections, acute consultations).

### 2.3 The Locus of Blood Pressure Recording
A comprehensive code audit across all modules in `config/modules/` revealed:
- [`config/modules/hypertension.yaml`](file:///Users/shashi/synapse/synapse-phase2/config/modules/hypertension.yaml) is the **sole module** that defines `observation_code: SBP` and `paired_observation_code: DBP`.
- `diabetes_t2.yaml` records only `HbA1c` and `Glucose`.
- `urti.yaml` generates consultation records but records no vital signs in `observations.csv`.
- Normotensive individuals in `hypertension.yaml` transition from `AgeCheck` to `NormotensiveDelay` (365 days) and loop back to `AgeCheck`. **They never enter `ScreeningEncounter` or `RecordBloodPressure`.**

### Critical Telemetry Findings:
1. **Hypertension Status is the Sole Determinant of Measurement:** In every single stratum, the number of measured individuals equals the number of hypertensive individuals exactly ($\text{Coverage} = 100\%$ among hypertensives, $\text{Coverage} = 0\%$ among normotensives).
2. **Measurement Probability Mirrors Age-Specific Prevalence:** The proportion of older adults measured increases in older age bands (e.g. $96.0\%$ in males 75+) because baseline hypertension prevalence increases with age in the demographic generator.
3. **Absence of Routine Well-Adult Screening:** In real-world Indian primary healthcare (e.g. Ayushman Bharat Comprehensive Primary Health Care), adults aged 30+ receive routine universal non-communicable disease (NCD) screening regardless of symptoms. In SYNAPSE, routine well-adult screening is not modeled; vital signs are captured strictly downstream of disease onset.

## 5. Distinction Between Population Characteristics and Healthcare Utilization

The audit confirms that the synthetic data generator conflates two fundamentally distinct concepts:
- **Epidemiological Population State:** The true underlying physiological state of synthetic individuals in the community (comprising both normotensives and hypertensives).
- **Healthcare Chart Capture:** The observational record generated when an individual presents to a clinic for a specific condition.

Because SYNAPSE Phase 2 was designed as a simulation of hospital electronic health records rather than a community health survey, only clinical events generate EHR rows.

---

## 6. Audit Verdict and Governance Recommendations

1. **Do Not Treat Measured Subset as Population-Representative:** The 235 measured older adults must be explicitly documented as an **outpatient hypertensive cohort**, not a representative sample of all older adults.
2. **No Population Screening Mechanism Created During Audit:** In compliance with audit restrictions, no ad-hoc population screening mechanism was invented or injected into the codebase.
3. **Proposed Design Recommendation (Requires Separate Scientific Approval):** If future phases of SYNAPSE require true population-level external validation against community surveys like LASI, an **Independent Universal Community Survey Layer** should be designed and calibrated against authoritative Indian survey protocols (such as NFHS-5 or LASI Field Manuals) under a dedicated Architecture Decision Record (ADR).
