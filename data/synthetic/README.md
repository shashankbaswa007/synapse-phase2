## 2. Relational Schema & Table Catalog (18 CSV Tables)

The dataset is normalized across 18 relational tables with unambiguous primary keys (`id` or `{table}_id`) and foreign keys referencing `patient_id` or `encounter_id`.

### 2.1 Patient Identity & Vital Records
- [`patients.csv`](patients.csv) — Demographics (National ID, birthdate, sex, state, urban/rural residence, race, marital status, lat/long).
- [`deaths.csv`](deaths.csv) — All-cause background mortality and clinical death records calibrated against Census SRS 2016–2020 life tables.

### 2.2 Clinical Care & Trajectories
- [`conditions.csv`](conditions.csv) — Active and historical chronic conditions (ICD-10 codes: `I10` essential hypertension, `E11.9` type 2 diabetes mellitus, `J06.9` acute upper respiratory infection).
- [`medications.csv`](medications.csv) — Prescriptions and stepped-care regimens (Amlodipine, Telmisartan, Metformin, Amoxicillin, Paracetamol).
- [`care_plans.csv`](care_plans.csv) — Outpatient chronic disease management protocols and lifestyle interventions.
- [`symptoms.csv`](symptoms.csv) — Patient-reported and clinician-elicited symptoms (e.g. headache, polyuria, cough).
- [`observations.csv`](observations.csv) — Clinical measurements recorded during healthcare encounters (vital signs, BMI, fasting glucose, clinic BP).
- [`encounters.csv`](encounters.csv) — Clinical encounter records, outpatient visits, diagnoses, and provider IDs.

### 2.3 Independent Community Health Survey (LASI Protocol)
- [`community_assessments.csv`](community_assessments.csv) — Household survey interviews for older adults ($\text{age} \ge 45$), decoupled from hospital visits.
- [`community_bp_readings.csv`](community_bp_readings.csv) — Standardized triplicate Omron-style BP measurements displaying authentic habituation drops (Reading 1 $\to$ 2 $\to$ 3).

### 2.4 Structural Schema Placeholders (Contractual Compatibility)
The following 7 tables are populated during discrete-event inpatient runs (`--mode hospital`) and are preserved here with valid RFC 4180 headers to satisfy relational schema contracts and integrity audits:
- [`invoices.csv`](invoices.csv) — Departmental billing and encounter invoice line items.
- [`payments.csv`](payments.csv) — Financial transaction records and insurance settlements.
- [`labs.csv`](labs.csv) — Specialized diagnostic laboratory panels (beyond routine clinic observations).
- [`radiology.csv`](radiology.csv) — Imaging orders, modalities (X-Ray, CT), and radiologist findings.
- [`events.csv`](events.csv) — Granular discrete-event engine simulation telemetry.
- [`journey_traces.csv`](journey_traces.csv) — Patient state transition journey traces.
- [`patient_timeline.csv`](patient_timeline.csv) — Inter-departmental transfer logs.
